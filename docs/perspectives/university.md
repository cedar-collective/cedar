# University Perspective

**For provosts, vice provosts, and central administration**

CEDAR provides confidence in the consistency and integrity of institutional analytics.

- Are key metrics defined the same way across units?
- Can results be traced and verified?
- Do institutional patterns reflect what is happening in programs and departments?

CEDAR strengthens institutional analytics by ensuring that it is built on transparent and reproducible foundations.

---

## What matters at the university level

- Consistency of definitions and metrics
- Traceability of results
- Alignment between institutional and local realities
- Ability to audit and reproduce analyses

---

## How CEDAR helps

- Shared, versioned analytic logic
- Inspectable code and methodology
- Reproducible results across terms and units
- Infrastructure for collaboration between IR, IT, and academic leadership
