# College Perspective

**For deans, associate deans, and college-level analysts**

CEDAR enables colleges to connect institutional patterns with program-level realities.

- Where do challenges emerge within specific programs?
- How do policies and structures shape outcomes across departments?
- Are interventions aligned with how students actually move through curricula?

CEDAR provides a way to move between aggregate insight and local detail without losing coherence.

---

## What matters at the college level

- Understanding cross-departmental trends
- Connecting policy to outcomes
- Supporting program review and accreditation
- Identifying where interventions are needed

---

## How CEDAR helps

- Flexible, code-based analyses adaptable to college needs
- Transparent logic for comparing programs and departments
- Tools for surfacing both broad patterns and local exceptions
