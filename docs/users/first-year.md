# First Year
{: .fs-9 }

**How much of each entering class is still here after its first semesters?**
{: .fs-6 .fw-300 }

---

First Year follows each entering class, the first of three pages: First Year, then [Retention](retention) for every enrolled student term by term, then [Graduation](graduation) for the end. It lives under **Bird's Eye > First Year**.

---

## Controls

The page builds as soon as it opens; there is no run button.

| Control | What it changes |
|---|---|
| **Entering term** | Fall or spring: the term the class entered in. |
| **Student level** | Undergraduate to start. |
| **Home campus** | The students' home campus in their entering term. It opens on the main campus; clear it to read every campus. |
| **Entering students** | Who a class holds: first-time students, first-time and full-time, new transfer students, or everyone new at this level. |

---

## Each Entering Class, Semester by Semester

One line for each of a class's 2nd, 3rd, 4th and 5th semesters, with a point for each entering class. The entering term is the 1st semester, so a year on is the 3rd, the one retention is usually quoted at. The gap between two lines is the share lost between them.

## Reading it

- **Still enrolled means enrolled in that later term on any campus, or awarded a degree by then.** A student away one term and back the next is missing from the first and counted in the second.
- **A first-time student is one the registrar's record calls first-time.** A student who took college courses in high school enters when the record says so, not at their first course. A first-time student who started in the summer and enrolled that fall is in the fall class, as federal reporting counts them.
- **A semester is a fall or a spring.** Summer is not counted as a step.
- **A line stops at the last class whose later term has settled.**
