# Shared bachelor requirements · 2026-2027

Readable paraphrases of the saved catalog rules. Transcribed does not mean advisor-approved.
Illustrative planned Gen Ed coverage and standard upper-division credit context. Not a student degree audit; approvals, transfer, grades and conditional alternatives are not evaluated.

## How the layers combine

- Combine the University, Gen Ed, awarding-college and degree-specific rule sets. Preserve each source and scope.
- A degree total is a minimum; applicable higher degree-specific totals still apply. Gen Ed/college/upper-division/residency constraints are overlapping subsets, not credits to add to that total.
- Satisfy all applicable policies; a stricter scope refines a general allowance. Do not silently overwrite conflicts or assume that one rule replaces another without evidence.
- A course may satisfy approved requirements in more than one layer, but earned credits count once. Flexible credits are additional Gen Ed coursework; do not fill them by reusing credits already allocated to a base area. Any other cross-area overlap requires explicit policy evidence.
- Apply only source-supported alternatives and waivers. Placement is not earned credit. Admission milestones, grades, approvals and transfer exemptions need evidence.
- Unallocated degree-credit capacity must not be labeled as an identified Gen Ed requirement. Scheduling preferences (15 usual / 16 maximum; early Gen Ed) remain separate from catalog requirements.

## University-wide bachelor requirements

Coverage: Selected catalog sections.

First bachelor degree completion and catalog-selection excerpts. Associate degrees, certificates, shared-credit graduate programs and second-degree policies are outside this set.
All thresholds constrain the same earned-credit record; they are not additive course blocks. These transcriptions do not evaluate a student.

- **Total degree credit** (`unm-university.degree_credits`): Earn at least 120 semester credits. A program can require a higher total. Source: [Graduation Requirements](https://catalog.unm.edu/#/policies/r1rjob8mj).

- **Admission to awarding college** (`unm-university.awarding_college`): Be admitted to the degree-awarding college at graduation. Source: [Graduation Requirements](https://catalog.unm.edu/#/policies/r1rjob8mj).

- **General Education** (`unm-university.gen_ed`): Complete the applicable UNM General Education requirements; see unm-general-education. Source: [Graduation Requirements](https://catalog.unm.edu/#/policies/r1rjob8mj).

- **University cultures requirement** (`unm-university.cultures`): Complete an approved 3-credit Critical Analyses of US and Global Cultures course with at least C. The requirement may overlap eligible Gen Ed or program coursework. Source: [Critical Analyses of US and Global Cultures Requirement for Undergraduate Degrees](https://catalog.unm.edu/#/policies/Hk-IsW8Qo).

- **Cultures overlap** (`unm-university.cultures_overlap`): An approved cultures course can also meet Gen Ed or program requirements when it meets their conditions; count its earned credits once toward the degree total. Source: [Critical Analyses of US and Global Cultures Requirement for Undergraduate Degrees](https://catalog.unm.edu/#/policies/Hk-IsW8Qo).

- **UNM residence** (`unm-university.resident_credits`): Earn at least 30 credits at UNM, excluding extension and correspondence independent-study credits. Source: [Graduation Requirements](https://catalog.unm.edu/#/policies/r1rjob8mj).

- **Senior residence** (`unm-university.senior_residence`): Of the UNM residence credits, earn at least 15 after accumulating 92 earned credits. They need not be the final credits of the degree; summer attendance may qualify. Source: [Graduation Requirements](https://catalog.unm.edu/#/policies/r1rjob8mj).

- **Cumulative GPA** (`unm-university.gpa`): Have a cumulative GPA of at least 2.0. Source: [Graduation Requirements](https://catalog.unm.edu/#/policies/r1rjob8mj).

- **English writing competence** (`unm-university.writing`): Complete ENGL 1120 with at least C, or meet the authorized proficiency-test alternative. A placement waiver does not itself award course credits. Source: [Graduation Requirements](https://catalog.unm.edu/#/policies/r1rjob8mj).

  - **Writing course** (`unm-university.writing_course`): ENGL 1120 with C or better. Source: [Graduation Requirements](https://catalog.unm.edu/#/policies/r1rjob8mj).

  - **Authorized proficiency** (`unm-university.writing_proficiency`): Qualifying score on an authorized writing proficiency test before graduation. Source: [Graduation Requirements](https://catalog.unm.edu/#/policies/r1rjob8mj).

- **CR/NC limit** (`unm-university.crnc`): Apply no more than 24 credits taken under the CR/NC grading option; college and program restrictions also apply. Source: [Graduation Requirements](https://catalog.unm.edu/#/policies/r1rjob8mj).

- **Extension and correspondence limit** (`unm-university.extension`): Apply no more than 40 extension/correspondence credits, of which no more than 30 may be correspondence credits. Source: [Graduation Requirements](https://catalog.unm.edu/#/policies/r1rjob8mj).

- **Major residence** (`unm-university.major_residence`): Complete at least half the minimum major credits as UNM class or laboratory work. A senior-transfer exception needs major-department approval and can reduce this to one quarter. Source: [Graduation Requirements](https://catalog.unm.edu/#/policies/r1rjob8mj).

- **Minor residence** (`unm-university.minor_residence`): When completing a minor, earn at least one quarter of its minimum credits as UNM class or laboratory work. Source: [Graduation Requirements](https://catalog.unm.edu/#/policies/r1rjob8mj).

- **Unresolved grades** (`unm-university.unresolved_grades`): Resolve all I and NR grades by the published end of the graduation semester. Source: [Graduation Requirements](https://catalog.unm.edu/#/policies/r1rjob8mj).

- **Graduation application** (`unm-university.graduation_application`): Apply for degree candidacy and contact the college before the last semester. College deadlines may be earlier. Source: [Graduation Requirements](https://catalog.unm.edu/#/policies/r1rjob8mj).

- **Catalog selection** (`unm-university.catalog_selection`): Normally use the catalog in effect on admission to the degree-granting college; an internal college/program transfer uses the catalog in effect at transfer, subject to University changes. Source: [General Regulations](https://catalog.unm.edu/#/policies/HkCl5ZLXo).

- **Readmission catalog** (`unm-university.readmission_catalog`): An interruption of three or more consecutive semesters, counting summer, requires the catalog in effect on re-enrollment. Source: [General Regulations](https://catalog.unm.edu/#/policies/HkCl5ZLXo).

- **Ten-year catalog limit** (`unm-university.ten_year_catalog`): A student taking more than ten years from original admission follows the catalog in effect in the intended graduation semester. Source: [General Regulations](https://catalog.unm.edu/#/policies/HkCl5ZLXo).

**Source question — cultures-naming:** The current cultures policy and Gen Ed program use Critical Analyses of US and Global Cultures; the graduation policy retains the earlier Diversity, Equity, Inclusion and Power wording. Preserve this naming discrepancy and verify the approved list; do not create two additional 3-credit requirements.

- **Gen Ed grade** (`unm-general-education.grades`): Require C or better for letter-graded Gen Ed work. CR/NC use is subject to University, college and department rules. Source: [General Education Curriculum](https://catalog.unm.edu/#/policies/rkd45WU7o).

- **Overlap with major** (`unm-general-education.overlap`): A listed major course may also satisfy its eligible Gen Ed area. Allocate only approved overlaps and count earned degree credits once. Source: [General Education Curriculum](https://catalog.unm.edu/#/policies/rkd45WU7o).

- **Program-specific restrictions** (`unm-general-education.restrictions`): College and department requirements may restrict which otherwise-approved Gen Ed courses a student can use. Source: [General Education Curriculum](https://catalog.unm.edu/#/policies/rkd45WU7o).

- **Completed New Mexico Gen Ed transfer** (`unm-general-education.transfer`): Verified completion of another New Mexico institution’s Gen Ed program normally satisfies UNM Gen Ed unless the student opts into UNM Gen Ed. The University writing and cultures requirements still apply. Incomplete transferred Gen Ed follows UNM requirements and advising. Source: [General Education Curriculum](https://catalog.unm.edu/#/policies/rkd45WU7o).

- **Exam and substitution credit** (`unm-general-education.exam_credit`): AP, IB and CLEP may apply subject to the Admissions alternative-credit rules. Department/college substitutions and exceptions require approval; this bundle does not award such credit automatically. Source: [General Education Curriculum](https://catalog.unm.edu/#/policies/rkd45WU7o).

- **Early math and communication** (`unm-general-education.early_math_writing`): Completing Communication and Mathematics/Statistics in the first year is catalog guidance, not a fixed semester prerequisite or additional credit requirement. - **Gen Ed and cultures** (`unm-arts-sciences.gen_ed`): Complete University Gen Ed and the University cultures requirement; use the shared University/Gen Ed sets rather than adding duplicate requirements here. Source: [College of Arts and Sciences](https://catalog.unm.edu/#/content/68a34618f6461f0934cc1580).

**Source question — honors-treatment:** The college page states that 18 Honors credits count as A&S credit but does not fully specify the interpretation of that allowance in this passage. ## School of Engineering bachelor rules

Coverage: Selected catalog sections.

Selected major-admission and bachelor-completion policies. Department-specific rules can be stricter. Upper-division credit totals are program-specific; A&S rules do not apply.

- **Major-status degree-applicable credit** (`unm-engineering.entry_credits`): Complete at least 26 credits applicable to the intended degree. Source: [School of Engineering](https://catalog.unm.edu/#/content/68a34619f6461f0934cc1584).

- **Technical admission coursework** (`unm-engineering.entry_technical`): The page states at least 18 first-year technical credits and separately completion of the 19–25 technical credits in the first-year curriculum. Technical GPA is at least 2.5 and each technical grade at least C-minus; verify the applicable program threshold. Source: [School of Engineering](https://catalog.unm.edu/#/content/68a34619f6461f0934cc1584).

- **Admission GPA** (`unm-engineering.entry_gpa`): Meet at least a 2.30 GPA across coursework presented for major admission, plus the separate technical-course GPA rule. Programs can be stricter. Source: [School of Engineering](https://catalog.unm.edu/#/content/68a34619f6461f0934cc1584).

- **Admission writing** (`unm-engineering.entry_writing`): Complete ENGL 1110 or its accepted equivalent. Do not infer legacy course equivalences from their labels. Source: [School of Engineering](https://catalog.unm.edu/#/content/68a34619f6461f0934cc1584).

- **Engineering GPA** (`unm-engineering.degree_gpa`): Have at least a 2.0 GPA on UNM work counted toward the degree and at least a 2.0 GPA on all UNM work. Source: [School of Engineering](https://catalog.unm.edu/#/content/68a34619f6461f0934cc1584).

- **Required-course grades** (`unm-engineering.course_grades`): Unless a stricter rule applies, required curriculum courses need at least C-minus. Gen Ed courses need at least C. This is a catalog eligibility rule, independent of CEDAR’s analytical DFW definition. Source: [School of Engineering](https://catalog.unm.edu/#/content/68a34619f6461f0934cc1584).

- **Required-course attempts** (`unm-engineering.attempt_limit`): Complete required graduation coursework within three attempts, including external courses. The source counts letter grades, W, CR, NC, I and audit as attempts. Source: [School of Engineering](https://catalog.unm.edu/#/content/68a34619f6461f0934cc1584).

- **Low-grade credit restriction** (`unm-engineering.low_grade_hours`): Students with 30 or more credits of D-plus, D, D-minus, F or NC cannot graduate in Engineering, except work removed through the applicable academic-renewal or repeat policy. Source: [School of Engineering](https://catalog.unm.edu/#/content/68a34619f6461f0934cc1584).

- **Physical education activity** (`unm-engineering.excluded_pe`): Physical education activity courses do not count toward Engineering bachelor requirements. Source: [School of Engineering](https://catalog.unm.edu/#/content/68a34619f6461f0934cc1584).

- **Introductory Studies** (`unm-engineering.excluded_intro`): Introductory Studies courses do not count toward Engineering bachelor requirements. Source: [School of Engineering](https://catalog.unm.edu/#/content/68a34619f6461f0934cc1584).

- **Gen Ed course restrictions** (`unm-engineering.gen_ed`): Complete University Gen Ed using any specific courses required by the Engineering program/accreditation curriculum. Source: [School of Engineering](https://catalog.unm.edu/#/content/68a34619f6461f0934cc1584).

- **Engineering degree application** (`unm-engineering.degree_application`): File the degree application with the department during the second semester of junior year and no later than completing 100 degree-acceptable credits. Source: [School of Engineering](https://catalog.unm.edu/#/content/68a34619f6461f0934cc1584).

**Source question — technical-admission-threshold:** The same school page uses 18 technical credits and 19–25 first-year technical credits. Preserve both statements and require program-specific clarification before evaluating admission.
